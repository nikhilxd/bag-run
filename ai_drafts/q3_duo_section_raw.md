## Duo Evolutions and Echo Summon Flow

Echoes leverages asynchronous multiplayer to deliver a seamless social experience in every run. At its core, the system lets players summon a ghost version of another player's build, creating exciting synergies and a robust sense of community.

**Summoning.** Players can summon an Echo at any time during a run. The Echo is chosen from friends, guildmates or top players. The Echo fights for 30 seconds with the full power of the original build, taking damage and dealing damage like a real ally. If the Echo is defeated it disappears.

**Tags.** Every weapon has tags such as Fire, Frost, Lightning, Orbit and Area. When the player and the Echo share a tag, a Duo Evolution is triggered, boosting both builds by 50% damage for the rest of the run.

**Duo Evolutions.**
1. Fire + Fire: Inferno. Massive fire damage.
2. Frost + Frost: Blizzard. Freezes everything.
3. Fire + Frost: Steam. Creates a damaging cloud.
4. Lightning + Orbit: Storm Ring. Orbiting lightning.
5. Area + Area: Shockwave. Big explosions.
6. Summon + Summon: Legion. More minions.
7. Orbit + Orbit: Halo. Bigger orbit.
8. Projectile + Fire: Rocket. Fiery projectiles.

**Rewards.** The owner of the Echo receives Thanks every time their Echo is used. Thanks can be spent in the shop.

**Edge cases.** If a player has no friends, a random Echo is used.
